    What this result does not prove

      OriginScope reports probabilistic forensic evidence. It does not establish authorship, legal ownership, or whether a caption/claim about an image is true.

      Evidence philosophy

      Metadata is a claim. Pixels are another witness.

      OriginScope keeps unsigned EXIF separate from pixel inference and cryptographic provenance. Missing metadata does not mean AI; camera metadata does not prove authenticity.

      Research direction

      From classification toward evidence infrastructure.

      The current release verifies image-origin evidence. The same architecture can later support capture-time provenance and auditable evidence chains without changing the principle: show what can be inferred, what can be cryptographically proven, and where uncertainty remains.
