Matrix computation debugger

Multiply two matrices and click into the arithmetic conceptually: inspect the exact row-by-column scalar products that create a selected output cell.

MatrixTrace exposes the individual products contributing to C[i,j], making matrix multiplication easier to audit, teach, and debug.
