Core-Norm

Bounded, asymmetric and invertible preprocessing for numeric machine-learning features—designed to limit the leverage of extreme values while retaining recoverable information about their magnitude.

Python · scikit-learnBrowser implementationGitHub-backed evidenceMIT licensed

HOW IT WORKS

Separate stable geometry from excess magnitude.

Core-Norm fits a median, separate lower/upper quartile scales, and a capped transition threshold on training data. It returns a bounded central coordinate and a bounded, monotone residual coordinate with a closed-form inverse.

01

Robust centre

Median-based centering reduces leverage from extreme observations.

m = Q₀.₅₀(x)
02

Asymmetric spread

Lower and upper dispersion are estimated separately.

s⁻ = m-Q₀.₂₅ · s⁺ = Q₀.₇₅-m
03

Central coordinate

The main coordinate is bounded even for arbitrarily extreme finite inputs.

C = clip(u/τ, -1, 1)
04

Residual coordinate

Tail magnitude is compressed monotonically rather than discarded.

R = sign(u) · log(1+d)/(1+log(1+d))
