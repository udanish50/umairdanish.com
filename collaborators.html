Collaboration principles

Strong collaborations make the evidence stronger.

01Define the real question

Technical objectives are aligned with the human, scientific, or domain decision the work must support.

02Design evidence early

Baselines, outcomes, validation criteria, and interpretation plans are established before conclusions are drawn.

03Build reproducibly

Code, assumptions, datasets, and failure modes are made explicit so results can be examined and extended.
