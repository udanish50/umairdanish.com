RESEARCH

Machine learning that can be examined, tested, and used with confidence.

I develop machine-learning methods that combine interpretability, domain knowledge, and robust representation learning, with applications in energy systems, perception, finance, medical imaging, and human-centered AI.

01

Understanding how predictive information is represented and how explanations can support human reasoning.

How can internal neural representations become useful evidence without changing the deployed model?

My work includes mechanistic analysis, concept probing, finite-difference explanation, representation visualization, and human-centered evaluation of explanations.

RESEARCH PRACTICE

From question to evidence.

  1. Question firstDefine the scientific and human question before selecting the model.
  2. Strong baselinesCompare with transparent baselines, relevant contemporary methods, and meaningful ablations.
  3. Multiple forms of evidenceCombine predictive metrics with robustness tests, statistics, visual diagnostics, and user evidence where appropriate.
  4. Reproducible outputsDocument code, assumptions, figures, and experimental choices so results can be inspected and extended.
