Research

Building AI systems that reveal enough evidence to be audited and improved.

My research combines model design, mechanistic analysis, human-centered evaluation, and reproducible experimentation across high-impact domains.

A unified research program

The central question across my work is: how can high-performing neural systems remain transparent enough for people to understand their behavior, identify failure modes, and make responsible decisions?

Design models that respect data structure and domain constraints.

Explain predictions through internal representations and faithful evidence.

03

Evaluate performance, robustness, cognition, and human alignment.

04

Release reproducible code, analyses, and research artifacts.

01
Mechanistic interpretability & XAI

Understanding what neural networks know, where they encode it, and how it shapes decisions.

I develop non-interventional approaches that expose predictive information across internal representations without modifying the deployed model. The objective is to move from post-hoc visualizations toward faithful, auditable evidence about computation.

Research practice

How I conduct research

Publication-grade work requires more than a new architecture. I build evidence through controlled baselines, ablations, statistical analysis, failure inspection, and reproducibility.

Rigorous experimental design

Clear hypotheses, realistic splits, controlled baselines, ablations, subgroup analysis, and uncertainty-aware interpretation.

Reproducible implementation

Version-controlled Python and PyTorch pipelines, documented preprocessing, fixed evaluation protocols, and reusable artifacts.

Human-centered evidence

Explanations and metrics evaluated against user roles, perception, cognition, and the real cost of errors.

Interdisciplinary translation

Methods and software developed with collaborators across engineering, computer science, music, health, finance, and visual media.
