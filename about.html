ABOUT

Muhammad Umair Danish, PhD

Postdoctoral Fellow in Applied AI · Western University

Muhammad Umair Danish is a Postdoctoral Fellow in Applied AI at Western University. His research focuses on explainable and interpretable artificial intelligence, reliable temporal learning, and the evaluation of machine-learning systems in applied settings.

He completed a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Western University, where his dissertation examined neural networks and their interpretability for building-energy modeling. His work has since expanded across mechanistic interpretability, human-centered explainable AI, physics-guided learning, time-series forecasting, perceptual evaluation of AI-generated images, and research software.

Across these areas, he is interested in a common question: how can machine-learning systems provide evidence that is technically meaningful and useful to people? His research combines model development with careful baselines, ablations, statistical validation, visualization, and human-centered evaluation where appropriate. He currently works under the supervision of Dr. Katarina Grolinger and collaborates with researchers across computing, engineering, health, and music therapy.
