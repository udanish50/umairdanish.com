UPLOAD CHECKLIST

1. Open https://github.com/udanish50/umairdanish.com
2. Click "uploading an existing file" or Add file -> Upload files.
3. Extract the ZIP on your computer first.
4. Drag ALL extracted files and folders into GitHub. Hidden folders such as .github must also be included.
5. Commit directly to the main branch.
6. Open Settings -> Pages -> Source -> GitHub Actions.
7. Wait for the Pages workflow to finish under the Actions tab.
8. Do not change Porkbun DNS until the GitHub Pages deployment is successful.
