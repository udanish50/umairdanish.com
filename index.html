Research architecture

Four connected layers of trustworthy AI.

My research moves from the internal mechanics of neural models to the human consequences of their explanations and predictions.

01

Mechanistic interpretability

Tracing concepts, representations, and predictive signals through neural networks without altering the model.

02

Human-centered XAI

Testing whether explanations support understanding, appropriate trust, and decision quality.

03

Reliable temporal learning

Building adaptive and physics-guided models for heterogeneous real-world time series.

04

AI evaluation

Aligning computational metrics with human perception and application-level evidence.
