The person behind the work

Research is most useful when it stays connected to people.

I am an AI researcher who enjoys the full path from a difficult question to an experiment, a reproducible result, and something another person can actually try. My work is technical, but the goal is practical: make intelligent systems more understandable, reliable, and useful.
